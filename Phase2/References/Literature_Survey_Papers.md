# Literature Survey — IEEE Papers

All 20 papers cited in the project report are **IEEE publications** (IEEE journals, IEEE conferences or IEEE/CVF
conferences), and each was checked against its official record. The presentation uses 15 of them.

Each title links to the paper's official page. Open-access papers download directly; the others can be downloaded
from IEEE Xplore through an institutional login.

**Columns:** *Report* and *PPT* give the reference number used in the project report and in the presentation.
